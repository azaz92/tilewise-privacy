TileWise Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 November 2026

TileWise is a calculator app for tile, paint, flooring, drywall and concrete. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. The short version: TileWise does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal data.

Information we collect

None. TileWise has no account or sign-up, and it does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information.

Information stored on your device

The numbers you type into the calculators, your US or Metric choice and the last screen you used are saved only on your own device, so they are still there the next time you open the app. This information never leaves your device. You can remove it at any time with the Clear button in the app, by clearing the app's storage in Android settings, or by uninstalling the app.

Internet access

TileWise does not request internet access from Android, so it cannot send any information off your device.

Advertising, analytics and third parties

TileWise contains no advertising, no analytics or tracking tools, and shares no information with third parties.

Children

TileWise is a general-audience tool. It does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of TileWise changes how information is handled (for example by adding ads), this page will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.
